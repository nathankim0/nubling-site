Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Nubling is published by JinYeob Kim ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Your habits, check-ins, streaks, pets, items and settings are saved in the app. This content is stored only on your device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives it, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app or use "Erase all data" in Settings, it is removed.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app activity related to ads, and diagnostic data. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. On iOS, tracking for personalized ads only happens if you allow it in the system prompt. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We receive confirmation that a purchase is active on your device, not your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device at the time you choose. No push server is involved.

Children

Nubling is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your content: Settings > Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Limit ad personalization: Settings > Ad privacy choices where available, or your device's ad settings.
  • Turn off reminders: Settings in the app or the system notification settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

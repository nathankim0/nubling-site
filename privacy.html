Nubling

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Nubling is published by JinYeob Kim ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Your habits, check-ins, streaks, pets, items and settings are saved in the app. This content is stored on your device. Unless you turn on Backup (below), it never leaves the device and we cannot see it. If you delete the app or use "Erase all data" in Settings, it is removed from the device.

Optional account and backup

Signing in is optional; the app works fully without it. If you sign in with Apple or Google under Settings > Backup, we receive an account identifier and the email address the provider shares (with Apple you can hide your real address). We then store a copy of your habits, check-ins, pets and in-app rewards so you can restore it on another device. The backup is kept on servers operated for us by Supabase in Japan (Tokyo region), is encrypted in transit, and is accessible only to your account. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not read it except when you ask us for support.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Backup > Delete account. This deletes the account, the backup and any stored files from our server right away; records on your device stay. Signing out stops further backups.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, app activity related to ads, and diagnostic data. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. On iOS, tracking for personalized ads only happens if you allow it in the system prompt. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment details. Apple and Google send us notices about subscription events (for example a new subscription, a renewal or a refund) with the product, price, currency, country and a transaction number, which we keep to operate the service and keep financial records. These notices do not include your name or email.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device at the time you choose. No push server is involved.

Children

Nubling is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete your content on the device: Settings > Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete your account and backup: Settings > Backup > Delete account, or email us.
  • Limit ad personalization: Settings > Ad privacy choices where available, or your device's ad settings.
  • Turn off reminders: Settings in the app or the system notification settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

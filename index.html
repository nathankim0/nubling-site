Nubling

Small habits grow a friend

Nubling is a habit tracker with a pet that grows when you keep your habits. Check off your day, keep a streak, open chests and watch your friend evolve through four stages.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to contact@bible-habit.com. We read every message.

Subscriptions

Nubling Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. You can cancel at any time in your store account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
